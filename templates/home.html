Message from the Honourable Minister for Education

Honourable Aseri Masivou Radrodro

Minister for Education

Bula Vinaka and warm greetings to everyone.

It is a pleasure to join you for the launch of the Fiji Head Teachers Association website, an important step towards strengthening communication, collaboration and professional engagement among head teachers and education stakeholders across Fiji.

This website provides a modern platform for the Association to share information, promote professional development, highlight achievements and keep its members connected, regardless of where they serve.

“Leading with Vision, Empowering Schools, Enriching Lives, Shaping Fiji’s Future.”

This theme reflects the important role our head teachers play in our educational system.

Our head teachers are at the forefront of turning national education priorities into meaningful outcomes at the school level. Their leadership, commitment and dedication are essential to strengthening the quality of education in Fiji.

As we embrace digital transformation, I encourage the Fiji Head Teachers Association to use this platform to strengthen its professional network and share the good work being done by school leaders across the country.

The Ministry of Education remains committed to working alongside the Association and all education stakeholders to strengthen school leadership and improve learning outcomes for every child.

I congratulate the Fiji Head Teachers Association on this important milestone and wish the Association every success as it continues to lead with vision, empower schools, enrich lives and shape Fiji’s future.

HONOURABLE ASERI MASIVOU RADRODRO

Minister for Education
