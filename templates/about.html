About the Association

Fiji Primary School Principals Association

A professional network for primary school principals focused on advocacy, collaboration, school leadership, and the continuous improvement of education in Fiji.

Who We Are

A strong voice for primary school leadership

FPSPA exists to bring primary school principals together around shared priorities: effective school leadership, practical support, policy engagement, and better learning environments for children across Fiji.

The Association has grown from a small group of committed educators into a nationally recognized professional body. It continues to strengthen school leadership, improve service delivery, and contribute to the overall development of education in Fiji.

Advocacy

Representing principal perspectives in national education discussions.

Innovation

Support

Creating a trusted network of peer support across Fiji.

FPSPA's identity is carried by the leaders who serve schools, communities, and children across Fiji.

Historical Overview

From district beginnings to national service

The Association's history reflects decades of principal leadership, district collaboration, and renewed national commitment to professional support for Heads of Schools.

Early formation

In the early 1980s, around 1980-1981, the late Chattar Pal, originally from Ba and then serving as a Head Teacher in Suva, initiated discussions that led to the formation of the Ba association for school leaders. Several senior Head Teachers from Ba strongly supported the initiative.

First national conference

The first national conference was held at the former Nasinu Teachers College Hall from 18-19 November 1982. Annual conferences continued successfully until 1987.

Continuity and revival

After the events of 1987, many senior members resigned or migrated, affecting continuity. Despite this, committed Head Teachers sustained the work and helped maintain and later revive the Ba branch. In 1988, Mr. Pal was appointed as an Education Officer and stepped back from active participation.

Revival and Expansion

Renewed leadership in the West

In 1996, after his appointment as Head Teacher of Votualevu Public School, Mr. Pal, with support from fellow educators, played a significant role in reviving the Nadi association for school leaders. That revival inspired similar efforts across other districts.

The Western districts later formed a regional association for school leaders and organized several conferences. These initiatives contributed to the full revival of the national Association.

Mr. Pal went on to serve as President at district, regional, and national levels before becoming Divisional Education Officer Western.

Growth and Current Status

A professional body for Heads of Schools

From humble beginnings, FPSPA has developed into a strong professional body representing Heads of Schools across Fiji. The Association continues to evolve with member needs, maintain collaboration with the Ministry of Education, and enhance service delivery and professional support systems.

Mission and Vision

Leadership that lifts schools and communities

FPSPA promotes principled leadership, strong school governance, and a collaborative professional culture for primary school leaders.

Mission

To support, represent, and empower primary school principals through advocacy, professional growth, and practical partnership.

Vision

A confident and connected community of school leaders driving excellent education outcomes across Fiji.

Values

Integrity, service, stewardship, professionalism, and shared leadership.

What We Do

How the association serves principals

  • Represent and advocate for Heads of Schools at district, divisional, and national levels.
  • Address professional concerns and grievances of members.
  • Promote effective school leadership and management practices.
  • Promote development opportunities, forums, and practical learning resources.
  • Strengthen area and branch connections so principals remain supported locally.
  • Support Ministry of Education initiatives and policies.
