# FHTA Website User Manual

Version: 1.0  
Project: Fiji Head Teachers Association Website
Audience: Members, staff users, administrators, and content managers

---

## 1. Introduction

The FHTA website is a member and staff portal for the Fiji Head Teachers Association. It brings together association news, events, membership profiles, representatives, documents, helpdesk support, and online training/workshops in one system.

The project supports two main user groups:

- **Members**: Primary school head teachers and association members who use the website to access news, events, documents, representatives, helpdesk support, and training.
- **Staff/Admin Users**: FHTA staff or administrators who manage content, documents, events, helpdesk tickets, members, courses, workshops, certificates, and QR attendance.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Accessing the Website

Open the FHTA website in your browser. The top navigation gives access to public pages such as:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Resources
- News
- Training
- Contact

When you log in, the system also displays the **FHTA Portal sidebar**, making it easier to move between the main apps.

### 2.2 Creating an Account

1. Click **Create Account**.
2. Enter the required account details.
3. Submit the registration form.
4. After registration, complete your member profile when prompted.

### 2.3 Logging In

1. Click **Login**.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. After login:
   - Members are taken to the **Member Dashboard**.
   - Staff/Admin users are taken to the **Staff Dashboard**.

### 2.4 Logging Out

Click **Logout** in the top navigation or portal menu when you have finished using the system.

## 12. Training and Workshops

The training app manages FHTA courses, lessons, quizzes, workshops, attendance, and certificates.

### 12.1 Browsing Courses as a Member

1. Open **Training Catalog**.
2. Browse available courses and workshops.
3. Open a course to view description, learning outcomes, modules, lessons, schedules, and enrollment options.

### 12.2 Enrolling in Training

1. Open a course.
2. Select an available schedule if required.
3. Click the enroll button.
4. The course appears under **Training & QR Attendance** or **My Learning**.

### 12.3 Completing Lessons

1. Open **Training & QR Attendance**.
2. Select a course.
3. Read lesson content.
4. Mark lessons complete as required.

### 12.4 Completing Quizzes

Some courses include quiz questions.

1. Open the training course from My Learning.
2. Complete all required lesson content.
3. Submit the quiz.
4. If you meet the passing score, the course can count toward completion.

### 12.5 Certificates

When training requirements are met, the system can issue a certificate.

Certificates may require:

- Lesson completion.
- Passing quiz score, if quiz questions exist.
- Workshop attendance, if a selected schedule requires attendance.

Members can view and print certificates from the training area.

## 13. QR Code Attendance for Seminars and Workshops

QR attendance is used for seminar/workshop check-ins.

### 13.1 Member QR Check-In

Members must be logged in before using QR check-in.

1. Log in to the website.
2. Open **Training & QR Attendance** or scan the QR code at the seminar.
3. If scanning a posted QR code, the check-in page opens.
4. The system confirms attendance for the relevant workshop schedule.
5. Attendance may contribute to course completion and certificate eligibility.

### 13.2 Staff QR Attendance Setup

1. Open **Training & Workshops** from the staff sidebar.
2. Open the relevant course.
3. Create or edit a schedule.
4. Ensure QR check-in is enabled.
5. Open the schedule attendance page.

### 13.3 Printing a QR Poster

1. Open **QR Attendance Setup** or the staff course list.
2. Open the schedule attendance page.
3. Select **Print QR Poster**.
4. Print the poster and place it at the seminar sign-in desk.

### 13.4 Downloading a QR Code

On the schedule attendance page, staff can:

- Download the QR code as a PNG.
- Copy the direct check-in link.
- Open the print-ready poster.

### 13.5 Manual Attendance

Staff can also mark attendance manually:

1. Open the schedule attendance page.
2. Review enrolled participants.
3. Set each participant status.
4. Add notes if needed.
5. Save attendance.

## 18. Troubleshooting

### 18.1 I Cannot Log In

- Check that the username and password are correct.
- Confirm the account has been created.
- Contact staff if the account needs review.

### 18.2 I Do Not See My Member Profile

- Complete the member profile form.
- Make sure your account is logged in as a member.
- Contact support if profile information is missing.

### 18.3 I Cannot Access Staff Pages

- Staff pages require staff/admin permissions.
- Log in with a staff account.
- Ask an administrator to check your role.

### 18.4 QR Check-In Does Not Work

- Confirm you are logged in.
- Confirm you are enrolled in the related training/workshop.
- Confirm the QR code is for the correct schedule.
- Confirm QR check-in is enabled for that schedule.
- Ask staff to mark attendance manually if needed.

### 18.5 I Cannot Upload Files or Images

- Confirm your account has staff permissions.
- Check the file type and size.
- Try a supported image format such as JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, or WEBP.

### 18.6 A Page Looks Different on Mobile

On mobile devices:

- Use the **Portal menu** button to open the sidebar.
- Scroll down to view dashboard cards.
- Use the top navigation drawer for public pages.

## 19. Security and Good Practice

- Log out after using a shared computer.
- Do not share passwords.
- Upload only approved FHTA documents and images.
- Avoid posting private member information in public news or event descriptions.
- Use helpdesk tickets for support issues instead of sharing sensitive information publicly.
- Review QR attendance lists after each workshop to ensure records are correct.
